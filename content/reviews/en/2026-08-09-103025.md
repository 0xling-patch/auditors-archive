---
title: "Public Audit Observations of Lingche's Personal Security Audit Website"
category: "Security Review"
---
The auditor conducted a public-information review of Lingche's personal security audit website to assess the transparency of services presented and the overview of professional practices. These observations are based on publicly available website content and did not involve an in-depth analysis of actual audit activities or internal processes.

**Observation 1: Audit Scope Definition and Methodology Transparency**

The auditor observed that, while the website sets out the intent to provide security audit services, its public documentation remains relatively high-level regarding the process for defining the specific audit scope commissioned by clients and the detailed methodologies applied to different audit types (for example: Web applications, networks, code review). Clear, standardized scope definitions and transparent methodologies are the foundation for ensuring audit results are repeatable, persuasive, and for building client trust. It is recommended that the site provide more granular scope-definition examples or templates and outline the key phases and techniques of the methodology used, to increase clarity and set client expectations.

**Observation 2: Audit Report Standards and Completeness of Remediation Recommendations**

The auditor also noted that, although the site indicates audit results will be delivered as reports, the public information does not sufficiently describe the specific structure and contents of those reports, including severity ratings, risk assessment methodology, and actionable remediation recommendations. Effective reports are critical for clients to understand risk and prioritize remediation. It is recommended to publish a high-level overview or an anonymized sample of a typical audit report structure, highlighting clear risk classifications and evidence-based practical remediation steps to assist clients in follow-up actions.
